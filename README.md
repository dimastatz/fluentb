## About The Project

# fluentb
fluentb makes you faster and clearer in every app you write in. Press a hotkey to dictate straight into any text field (Slack, Gmail, VS Code, Notes), or press another to rewrite what you’ve typed into clean, fluent, correctly styled text. Transcription runs locally with Whisper, so your voice never leaves your machine. Built in Rust, lives in your system tray, and stays out of your way.

Currently supports macOS, with Windows and Linux planned.
